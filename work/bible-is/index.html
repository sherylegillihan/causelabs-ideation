Digital Platform

Digital Platform For Faith Comes By Hearing

A digital ecosystem of the world’s largest media-rich scripture library.

Faith Linguistics

Challenge

Faith Comes By Hearing has a mission to record and distribute audio translations of scripture in all 6,800+ languages of the world. How might technology scale the impact of a 35-year-old ministry that had been using audio cassette tapes and CDs as its main tool for worldwide scripture distribution?

Solution

CauseLabs helped develop a digital ecosystem with a robust API library — the Digital Bible Platform — as the cornerstone of a network supporting distribution of digitized content to reach people with tools they already use daily: Android, iOS and Smart TV apps, ePub, internet and satellite radio, and web streaming.

Results

Our partnership with Faith Comes By Hearing, which began in 2008, culminated in the creation of the world's largest repository of scripture text, audio, and video, accessed through a simple, intuitive suite of apps for the Web, iPhone, iPad, Android, Radio and Smart TV. The organization's reach of 50 million people during its first 35 years of operation was extended to over 100 million in the first four years partnering with CauseLabs, and now exceeds 250 million. In 2015, Faith Comes By Hearing was trained and transitioned to manage and support their digital ecosystem independently.
