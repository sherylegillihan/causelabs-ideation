Chapter 1

Technology can be used as a force for good.

CauseLabs, PBC is a public benefit corporation that uses technology as a force for good. Our expertise is in web services and website management. While we work on a project basis, we believe in partnerships over projects.

People solve problems, and we know that the people within the organizations we serve often create the change we want to see. Technology is simply a means to amplify that impact.

We aim to use it to grow positive impact by delivering solutions for nonprofits, social enterprises, and other values-aligned businesses. We believe that through partnership, we can make a difference.

Chapter 4

Strategic partnerships that scale impact.

We build websites and web solutions that elicit engagement and accelerate growth. When we launch, we deliver easy-to-manage, easy-to-use experiences.

We start with a strategy phase on all projects, even if it's a smaller build that only needs one or two meetings to suss out the primary goals.

We use a human-centered, growth-driven approach, especially on custom web platforms. This process is crucial so that we can confirm our hunches before building large-scale digital ecosystems. It saves time and money and validates that we are solving the right problems.

Chapter 5

Passion fuels our fire.

We excel at providing solutions that scale organizations. With multiple projects that have reached millions of users, and won awards, we have the experience to take your project to the next level.

Whether we're designing digital strategies or developing a custom web platform, each step in our process is vital. This process includes Strategy, Design, Development, and Continuous Improvement. Each phase gives us the empathy and data needed to build effective solutions.

You may have already gone through this cycle on your own or with another partner. We'd love to be onboard with what you've accomplished and bring our experience to help offer new perspectives.

01

Strategy

Discover what's possible.

Getting Started

Each Strategy Phase starts with a virtual session where we go deep into the problem, the people, and the ecosystem. This informs what we tackle in our Workshop together.

Identifying Emergent Themes

Armed with information and empathy, we come together in a Workshop to engage in activities and interviews that create a slew of concepts to test and validate with real people.

Fine-tuning The Concepts

We combine the best concepts into a clear strategy that outlines next steps: what we need to build, how long it will take, and the investment of time and funding required to make it happen.

02

Design

Create with empathy; build upon experience.

UI/UX Design

With our technology expertise, we design intuitive and engaging experiences that solve human problems and create measurable results toward achieving your goals. Building on the Strategy Phase, we deliver mockups and clickable prototypes for your review and feedback.

Human-Centered & Growth-Driven

Our team is certified in Human-Centered Design and Growth-Driven Design. We always consider the users when creating a user interface, and we design experiences to be tested and tracked so we can continue to learn and achieve optimal results.

Creating A Blueprint

Developing a large digital product is not so different from architecting a building. Creating a blueprint for success helps us build a strong foundation and ensures we stay true to our mission and achieve our goals as the pieces continue to evolve.

03

Development

Implement sustainable solutions.

A Product That Highlights Your Organization

Your organization exists to solve a problem. Your service or product is likely unique and isn't well represented on off-the-shelf tools that are one size fits all. CauseLabs specializes in developing websites and web platforms specifically for your needs.

Impact Through Technology

Our goal is to help your organization get the most out of the technologies that improve your products, services, and systems. Whether it's building a responsive website, providing world-wide access to digital libraries, or reaching millions of users through an application ecosystem, our multi-disciplinary team takes pride in crafting the interface and the inner workings that make those systems work.

04

Continuous Improvement

Your partner for ongoing success.

Continuous Improvement Retainers

Your project may have a backlog of items that didn't make it into the release. Budget is one reason, but many features don't make sense until you have user feedback and data to uncover where to invest. A retainer gives you a set number of hours per month for strategy, data reporting, prioritization, and implementation.

Product Maintenance and Support

Your organization now owns a digital product, and that comes with an ongoing need for security, maintenance, and support. We offer care plans for transitioning from active development to maintenance and support, and can assist with handoff and planning for continued success.

Measuring and Improving Outcomes

Once a product has launched, there is much to learn from how users are engaging with it. We can help you enhance your product to capture the right data and create reports or analytics dashboards. We want to help you measure what matters.
