About CauseLabs

Compassion meets innovation.

We're a WordPress agency with 20+ years of experience, using technology as a force for good — and winning global awards for it along the way.

Our mission

CauseLabs' mission is to grow positive impact.

Our origin story Our PBC status

CauseLabs is a premiere WordPress agency with 20+ years of experience and multiple awards for its web app and website projects. Winning several global awards as a social impact company makes us proud, but also humbled by the amazing clients we get to work with every day. Our clients inspire our commitment to excellence and help us evolve to offer solutions that solve complex challenges and amplify the potential of web technologies.

We accomplish our mission by using technology as a force for good and designing sustainable business and nonprofit websites and web apps. We leverage over 20 years of experience and expertise in technology to help organizations scale their impact through strategy, website design, custom web development, and website care plans.

Technology is driving growth and transforming the globe. We believe it's a powerful tool for growing businesses, but also for equalizing the inequities of our world, providing opportunity, giving voice and dignity to those that need uplifting, educating our next generation of leaders, and helping us become better global citizens. Together with our clients and partners, we are using technology as a force for good.

A WordPress agency investing in the greater good.

Since 2003, CauseLabs has built award-winning websites, web apps, and custom web platforms to help organizations scale their impact. In 2014, we became a certified B Corporation, and in 2017 we restructured as a Public Benefit Corporation.
