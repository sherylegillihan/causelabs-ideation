Recession Proofing Your Nonprofit Website

By Michael Gillihan

Nonprofit organizations have always played a crucial role in our society, and the looming recession makes their work more important than ever. In the current climate, it's essential to find ways to increase your organization's financial sustainability, and one of the most critical steps you can take is to optimize your website for maximum impact.

In this article, we'll explore some strategies for recession-proofing your nonprofit website, including properly maintaining your website, highlighting positive impact, and improving conversions.

Maintain Your Website

Your website is the face of your nonprofit to the world and it's essential to keep it running smoothly. Here are some tips to ensure your site stays in top shape:

  1. Make regular backups — Even the most secure websites can fall victim to cyber-attacks or data loss. Make regular backups of your website and database, so you can quickly restore your site in case of an emergency.
  2. Keep your software up to date — Regularly updating your website's core software, plugins, and themes is essential to ensure it runs smoothly and is secure from threats.
  3. Perform regular security checks — Hackers are always looking for vulnerabilities to exploit. Regularly check your website's security and make sure your firewalls, virus scanners, and security plugins are up to date and configured correctly.

By investing in these core website maintenance practices, you can ensure that your nonprofit's website is always up and running smoothly and that your supporters' data is safe and secure.

Highlight Your Positive Impact

You have a powerful story to tell, and your website is an excellent platform for sharing your good work. Here are some strategies for highlighting your organization’s positive impact:

  1. Use powerful imagery — Visuals are an excellent way to showcase your nonprofit's impact. Use high-quality images and videos that showcase your work and tell your story.
  2. Use real stories — Share stories of the people whose lives your organization has touched. Use quotes, testimonials, and other authentic content to bring their stories to life.
  3. Use data to support your story — Track and share key data that demonstrates the impact of your work. Use infographics and other visual aids to make this data accessible and easy to understand.

Highlighting your positive impact and celebrating the people who have benefited from your work will help to build trust with your audience and encourage them to support your organization.

Improve Conversions

Finally, it's essential to convert website visitors into supporters of your cause. Your website should be designed to encourage visitors to take action, such as making a donation or signing up to volunteer. Therefore, it's critical to focus on conversion optimization to ensure that your website helps to drive program sustainability.

Here are some strategies for improving conversions:

  1. Use clear calls to action (CTAs) — Make it clear what action you want visitors to take on each page of your website. Use clear, concise language and a prominent CTA to encourage visitors to take action.
  2. Optimize donation forms — Make it easy for visitors to donate to your nonprofit. Optimize your donation forms to be user-friendly and easy to complete.
  3. Regularly analyze website metrics — Use website analytics to understand how visitors are interacting with your website. Use this data to improve your website's user experience and increase conversions.

Staying focused on improving conversions ensures that you maximize the impact of your website and helps to increase your donations, volunteers, and supporters.
