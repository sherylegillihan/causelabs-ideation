Using AI to Write More Resource Content for Your Website; A Faster Brain Dump

By Sheryle Gillihan

In 2015 a World Economic Forum report stated, “Technology is transforming the world.” At the time, there were many news stories on its negative influences. After reading that report, our team was all in, vowing to use technology as a force for good. Yet now we have to ask, is it safe, ethical or even viable to use AI to write more resource content for your website visitors?

Technology advances rapidly and continues to transform the world. It was over a decade ago when books were written about the exponential impact of technology. Well, that day has long since arrived. Among these advancements, one stands out as a game changer that we can't ignore any longer: artificial intelligence (AI). It is eradicating mundane tasks and replacing them with automation. And not the standard "rinse and repeat" forms of automation, but rather the expediting of creative tasks such as writing, creating images, and researching data to arrive at a viable data-backed conclusion. Since 2023, it has completely transformed the landscape of content ideation and creation.

Embracing the cutting-edge AI tools can enhance your business, and using AI to help with website content is an easy first step to engaging your audience. What's fascinating is how AI has ushered in a brand new role — the AI Prompt Engineer, fusing technical know-how with your innate creativity. While we don’t believe AI can replace humans or eliminate our jobs, we do believe that those who know how to use AI will start to displace those who do not.

Whether you're a professional seeking actionable insights or someone hoping to make a positive impact, this guide offers you a glimpse into the dynamic uses of AI-infused content creation. It also positions CauseLabs as a supportive ally, committed to empowering clients to navigate and innovate within this burgeoning field.

Understanding the Role of AI Prompt Engineers

The Emergence of AI Prompt Engineers

As AI continues to permeate various aspects of content creation, the need for AI Prompt Engineers becomes increasingly apparent. These tech-savvy creatives are charged with crafting prompts that coax AI into generating insightful and engaging content. Their role requires a blend of skills - a solid understanding of AI mechanisms, a knack for creative writing, and an intuitive sense for what engages audiences. By harnessing AI's computational power, they're poised to revolutionize the way we generate website content, making it more efficient, diverse, and impactful than ever.

Leveraging AI for Writing New Resource Content

Generating Ideas with AI

AI prompts can be powerful catalysts for content idea generation. They offer an almost endless array of concepts, allowing for content creation that's not only efficient but also richly diverse. By providing a unique combination of relevance and novelty, AI prompts can stimulate ideas for content across various industries and niches. For example, an AI prompt engineer working in healthcare might use prompts to generate blog topics related to new medical technologies or wellness tips.

Writing Engaging and Informative Content

AI-powered tools can greatly facilitate the crafting of engaging and informative content. They can provide structural guidelines, offer suggestions for sentence construction, and even assist with grammar and syntax. However, it's essential to remember that your audience is human. Therefore, the language you use should be tailored to resonate with them. By integrating AI's capabilities with a keen understanding of your audience's preferences, you can create content that informs, engages, and persuades.

Incorporating Human Expertise

Striking a Balance between AI and Human Input

AI has undeniably transformed the content creation process, but the human element remains irreplaceable. AI Prompt Engineers can add the necessary nuance, empathy, and context to AI-generated content, thereby enhancing its effectiveness. They form a crucial bridge between technology and humans, ensuring that the final output is not just accurate but also emotionally resonant and relevant. The marriage of AI-generated content with human input creates a harmonious blend that optimizes the reach and impact of your content.

Want more? 

Following our suggested action items, it's worthwhile to share some insights from our own exploration of AI tools. These resources, tested and vetted by CauseLabs, have proven beneficial in our quest for improved efficiency and innovation:

  • Grammarly: It’s been around for so long and we’ve used it as a trusted grammar checker. We don’t think of it as AI, but it is so powerful! Did you know it can also check for plagiarism?
  • Missinglettr: An integral part of our content calendar for the past few years, Missinglettr assists us in creating social media posts and scheduling campaigns for our blog content. It has become a dependable tool in our arsenal. We recently became an affiliate so we will get a small kickback if you sign up using our link. Thanks in advance if you do.
  • Lumen5: Initially tested pre-pandemic, Lumen5 has grown impressively alongside AI capabilities. Originally used to convert our blog posts into video scripts, its prowess in selecting fitting video clips makes it a handy multimedia tool.
  • Canva's Magic Design: An engaging addition to Canva's suite, Magic Design brings a playful twist to media creation. If you're already a Canva user, this is a feature you should certainly experiment with.
  • ChatGPT: ChatGPT has been the talk of the town, and we're happy to report it lives up to the hype. It’s not just about writing content. It is a powerful tool that can be used in a variety of ways. We're currently using the paid version, which grants us access to various beneficial plugin add-ons.
  • Dall-E 2: As another product of OpenAI, Dall-E 2 is the first text-to-image diffusion model that produced satisfactory results in our testing. Though it requires an understanding of AI Prompt Engineering, ChatGPT can provide substantial support in this area.
  • Midjourney: Offering the most compelling text-to-image diffusion model we've encountered, Midjourney excels in style mimicry and with trial and error we’ve learned how to forge our own artistic style that aligns better with our brand. Note, this tool does require a Discord account to use it.
  • Magical AI: True to its name, Magical AI is remarkable at analyzing multiple browser tabs and condensing data into a single spreadsheet - a game-changer for data handling.

There are so many others, but this is a pretty good starting point.

What's on our radar next? We're eagerly anticipating trying out ElevenLabs text-to-speech and Revoicer AI voice generator. We also received an announcement that Vimeo has a new AI tool. As we continue to embrace the ever-evolving AI landscape, we are committed to sharing our discoveries and insights with you, ensuring we grow and innovate together.
