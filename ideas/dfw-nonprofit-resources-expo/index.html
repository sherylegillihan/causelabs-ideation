CauseLabs Will See You at DFW Nonprofit Resources Expo

By CauseLabs

CauseLabs is thrilled to announce its membership with the North Texas Nonprofit Institute, host of the upcoming DFW Nonprofit Resources Expo scheduled to take place on June 18, 2024 at the Hurst Convention Center. The event, which is free to attend, promises a day of invaluable networking opportunities and the chance to discover mission-friendly business partners whose products and services are specifically tailored to support nonprofit organizations.

At the DFW Nonprofit Resources Expo, CauseLabs will be featured at Booth 49, offering attendees the opportunity to engage directly with our team and learn more about our innovative digital solutions designed to empower nonprofits and maximize their impact. As a Texas Public Benefit Corporation, CauseLabs is dedicated to driving positive social change through sustainable business practices, and our participation in this event underscores our commitment to supporting the nonprofit sector. Why sponsor the DFW Nonprofit Resources Expo?

"Collaborating with nonprofits presents a unique dynamic often overlooked by businesses, neglecting the substantial purchasing influence wielded by local community organizations. North Texas is home to over 26,000 nonprofits, offering abundant opportunities for businesses aligned with their missions to contribute by becoming local vendors of goods and services. Many nonprofits actively seek businesses that share their values, deliver top-notch services, and contribute to maintaining a thriving local economy."

"The DFW Nonprofit Resources Expo transcends the typical vendor fair, serving not only as an avenue for nonprofits to discover mission-aligned businesses but also as an opportunity for businesses to gain insights into the specific needs of nonprofits. It allows them to express their commitment to supporting local enterprises and aids organizations in uncovering avenues for positive community impact through collaboration with local businesses."

Read more about it on the North Texas Nonprofit Institute website and reserve your booth: https://www.ntxnonprofits.org/nonprofitexpo2024

About CauseLabs, PBC:

CauseLabs, PBC is a Texas Public Benefit Corporation and Certified B Corporation dedicated to leveraging technology as a force for good. With a focus on web services and a process for sustainable digital stewardship, CauseLabs empowers nonprofits and purpose-driven organizations to achieve their missions through innovative digital solutions. From digital strategy, custom web design and software development to maintenance and ongoing impact measurement and support, CauseLabs partners with clients to create meaningful change in communities worldwide.
