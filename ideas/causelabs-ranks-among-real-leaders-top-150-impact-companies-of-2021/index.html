CauseLabs in the Media Jan 28, 2021

CauseLabs Ranks Among Real Leaders® Top 150 Impact Companies of 2021

By CauseLabs

CauseLabs is humbled to be ranked on the Real Leaders Top 150 Impact Companies of 2021. Real Leaders, a premier source for recognizing organizations that are making a positive social and environmental impact, announced the newly selected winners of its 2021 “Top Impact Companies” from around the world, which include well-respected brands of all sizes from a variety of industries, such as game changers Tesla, Danone, and Patagonia.

“My excitement was beyond measure when I found out we were being recognized for the Real Leader award,” said Sheryle Gillihan, CEO and Co-Owner of CauseLabs. “It has been a dream since the inception of this award, and I will continue learning, growing, and following in the footsteps of these Real Leaders as I help lead our company in a way that aligns with our values and fulfills our mission of growing positive impact.”

“These top impact companies prove that businesses can thrive by being a force for good’ said Mark Van Ness, Founder of Real Leaders. “They are the Real Leaders of the New Economy” added Van Ness.

CauseLabs' executive team attended a special ceremony on January 27th, 2021 to honor the winners with key impact speakers featuring Seth Goldman, Chairman of Beyond Meat and a musical performance from Michael Franti, world-renowned musician and activist, and his partner Sara Agah Franti.
