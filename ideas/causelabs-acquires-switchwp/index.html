CauseLabs acquires SwitchWP, enhancing it's digital solutions

By Sheryle Gillihan

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

CauseLabs Acquires SwitchWP, Enhancing Digital Solutions with the WP Client Reports Plugin

Grandview, Texas – November 20, 2023: In a move that marks a significant expansion of its digital toolkit, CauseLabs is thrilled to announce the acquisition of SwitchWP, a notable player in the WordPress plugin arena. This acquisition is headlined by the inclusion of the WP Client Reports plugin, a game-changer in website maintenance reporting for digital agencies, freelancers, and website managers.

At CauseLabs, we're all about making technology work for people, not the other way around. The WP Client Reports plugin fits perfectly into this ethos. It's a tool that simplifies the complex, turning website maintenance data into easy-to-understand, actionable reports. This enhances our Website Care Plans. This also means more transparency and stronger relationships between agency clients and their customers.

“Our journey has always been about harnessing the power of technology to create positive change,” says Sheryle Gillihan, CEO of CauseLabs. “Bringing SwitchWP and WP Client Reports under the CauseLabs umbrella is a natural extension of this mission. We're excited about the transparency this provides to our clients and the possibilities this opens up for our growing list of agency partners.”

WP Client Reports is celebrated for its intuitive design and detailed insights. Under CauseLabs' guidance, the plugin will not only continue to receive robust support but will also see enhancements that keep it ahead of the curve in website maintenance technology.

For the existing SwitchWP family, we promise a seamless transition with the continued excellence in support you've grown accustomed to. We're committed to nurturing and growing WP Client Reports, ensuring it remains an indispensable tool in your digital toolkit.

Michael Gillihan, CTO of CauseLabs, adds, “Incorporating WP Client Reports into our offerings is a big win. It aligns with our desire to foster transparency while also giving our clients the data needed to help manage their digital presence more effectively and efficiently. We're eager to integrate this plugin into our services and extend its benefits to a broader audience.”

For more details about CauseLabs, SwitchWP and the WP Client Reports plugin, please visit https://www.causelabs.com/ and https://switchwp.com/

About CauseLabs:

We're a team of digital innovators with a heart. At CauseLabs, we partner with growing organizations to design, develop, and scale sustainable digital products that make a real difference. We believe in using technology as a force for good, creating solutions that improve lives and grow positive impact.

As a web agency, we offer a series of web services for small businesses and nonprofits including digital strategy, website design, web development, and ongoing website care plans.

About SwitchWP & WP Client Reports :

SwitchWP has carved a niche in the WordPress community for serving agencies with its specialized reporting plugins. Known for enhancing WordPress website management, SwitchWP has become a go-to resource for web professionals seeking reliable and efficient digital tools.

WP Client Reports is the perfect maintenance report builder plugin for agencies, freelancers and site maintainers who update their client’s sites on a weekly, monthly, or quarterly basis. The plugin tracks what updates have happened and records them daily.
