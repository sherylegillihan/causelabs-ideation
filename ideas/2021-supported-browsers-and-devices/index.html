2021 Supported Browsers and Devices

By CauseLabs

Our supported browsers and device list helps communicate what is covered in our quality assurance testing when CauseLabs delivers a new website or web application. The following is our 2021 supported browsers and device list:

Supported Browsers:

  • Chrome: latest version available
  • Safari: latest version available
  • Firefox: latest version available
  • Edge (Chromium) on Windows 10: latest version available

NOTE: Microsoft End of Life dates for IE11 and Edge Legacy are as follows:

  • IE11: August 17, 2021
  • Edge Legacy: March 9, 2021

Android Devices:

  • Samsung S10 / OS 10
  • Google Pixel 3 / OS 9
  • Google Pixel / OS 8 (As Required)

NOTE: Android OS 11 has been released and is currently available on some Pixel devices. Samsung is expected to start releasing OS 11 to their devices by year-end. We will keep an eye on the OS 11 market share, currently at 0.3% and once it exceeds 5% we will look at starting to include it into our test suite of devices.

iOS Devices:

  • iPhone 12 / iOS 14
  • iPhone XS / IOS 13
  • iPhone 8 / iOS 14
  • iPhone Mini / iOS 14
  • iPad Pro 10.5 / iOS 14
