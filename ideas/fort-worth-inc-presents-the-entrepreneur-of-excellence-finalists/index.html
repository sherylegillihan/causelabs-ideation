Fort Worth Inc. presents the Entrepreneur of Excellence Finalists

By CauseLabs

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE: 8/30/2022

Panther City Media Group
6777 Camp Bowie Blvd. Ste. 130
Fort Worth, Texas 76116

FORT WORTH, Texas – Fort Worth Inc. is proud to present the finalists for the Entrepreneur of Excellence Awards Program.

Fort Worth Inc.'s Entrepreneur of Excellence (EOE) awards program recognizes outstanding entrepreneurs whose vision, creativity, and integrity have made Fort Worth the premier place to do business. These individuals have distinguished themselves within their industries through their leadership, perseverance, ethical business practices, sales and profit growth, community involvement, and more.

Applicants for the program have been evaluated by an independent panel of judges against others in their category and selected as finalists through a competitive process. One winner will be chosen from each category and be awarded at a formal awards gala held at the Fort Worth Club on November 3. A special individual will also be selected by Fort Worth Inc. for the Supporter of Entrepreneurship award.

Finalists and winners will receive editorial exposure and publicity, including an interview in Fort Worth Inc.’s EOE cover story. Winners will be inducted into the Entrepreneur of Excellence Hall of Fame and receive a custom pair of Justin’s Ostrich Boots and a handsome awards trophy.
