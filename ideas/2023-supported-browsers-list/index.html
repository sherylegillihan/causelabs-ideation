2023 Supported Browsers List

By CauseLabs

Browser testing no longer means attempting to make a website look the same in browsers of different capabilities or on devices with different size screens. However, it does mean ensuring someone’s experience of a design should be appropriate to the capabilities of their browser or device.

Editor's Note (2026): This article has been superseded by our updated 2026 Supported Browsers Guide for Websites, which reflects current browser support recommendations and industry best practices.

View the 2026 Supported Browsers List

Our supported browsers list helps communicate what is covered in our quality assurance testing when CauseLabs delivers a new website or web application. We test our work only in current stable versions of major desktop browsers. We do not test in older versions unless agreed upon and estimated separately.

The following outlines our Browser / Device List for 2023 based on the statistics obtained from Global Stats StatCounter (https://gs.statcounter.com).

Supported Browsers

For desktop environments, we will test designs using the latest stable versions of:

  • Chrome
  • Safari
  • Firefox
  • Edge on Windows 11

For mobile devices, we will test your design using the latest versions of:

  • iOS Safari on iPhone and iPad
  • Android Google Chrome

We do not test specific Android devices, or other mobile browsers unless agreed upon and estimated separately.

If enhanced designs are needed to meet legacy browser or specific device requirements, please notify us and we will provide a separate estimate to meet those specifications.

Recommendations for specific device testing are listed below.

ANDRIOD DEVICES

  • Samsung A13 5G / OS 12 (Upgrade to OS 13 approximately Q1)
  • Google Pixel 6 / OS 12
  • Samsung S10 / OS 11
  • Google Pixel / OS 10

IOS DEVICES

  • iPhone 14 / iOS 16
  • iPhone 12 / IOS 15
  • iPhone 8 / iOS 14
  • iPhone Mini / iOS 15
  • iPad 10th Generation / iOS 16
  • iPad Pro 10.5 / iOS 15
